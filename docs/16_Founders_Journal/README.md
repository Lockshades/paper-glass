# Founder's Journal

This journal records strategic observations, hypotheses, and decisions that may not fit naturally into a formal specification. It is not a diary and should not contain secrets or unnecessary personal information.

## Entry template

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

### Problem or observation

### Evidence

### Decision or hypothesis

### Consequence

### Follow-up
```

## 2026-07-27 — From AI exam app to educational infrastructure

### Problem or observation

The project had expanded beyond a simple AI-powered exam website. The strongest direction combines CBT practice, verified academic content, AI tutoring, analytics, and eventually contributor and institutional workflows.

### Decision or hypothesis

Treat the product as educational infrastructure with a student-first first release. Treat the Founder’s Blueprint as the source of truth for the reasoning behind the build.

### Consequence

The initial implementation must prove the student learning loop before attempting the full marketplace or platform ecosystem.

### Follow-up

Validate the first exam, subject, student segment, content standard, and commercial model with evidence.

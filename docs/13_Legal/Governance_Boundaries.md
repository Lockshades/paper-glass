# Legal, Safety, and Governance Boundaries

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Requires qualified review  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-27

This is a product boundary document, not legal advice. Qualified counsel should review the final policies, contracts, and launch plan.

## Areas requiring review

- privacy and data protection obligations applicable to student data;
- age, parental consent, and safeguarding requirements;
- educational-content copyright and licensing;
- examination-body trademarks and affiliation claims;
- contributor and reviewer agreements;
- intellectual-property ownership and attribution;
- payment, refunds, and consumer-protection rules;
- AI disclosure and complaint handling;
- data retention, deletion, and export;
- institutional contracts and cross-border data processing.

## Product claims

Do not claim official examination affiliation, guaranteed scores, or guaranteed admission without a documented basis and permission. Describe practice as practice.

## Governance principles

- collect minimum necessary data;
- make important automated assistance explainable;
- provide correction and reporting paths;
- preserve provenance for educational content;
- separate business incentives from academic approval;
- keep an auditable record of material changes;
- apply stronger review to features that can affect a student's high-stakes decision.

## Launch gate

No public launch should proceed until privacy notices, terms, content rights, support ownership, incident escalation, and examination-specific claims have been reviewed.
